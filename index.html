OUR STORY • OUR VISION

Small Beginnings.
Extraordinary Futures.

Every great future begins with a seed of faith.

Grace Horizon International School was born from a God-given vision entrusted to its founders, Dr. Samuel and Nike Ajayi. What began as a seed of faith grew into a mission to nurture children in knowledge, character, excellence, and the fear of God.

At the heart of this vision is a powerful declaration from God's Word, given as a proclamation over the lives of the children entrusted to the school.

“

The seed of the righteous shall be mighty.

PSALM 112:2

These words became more than a school motto. They became a declaration of faith, purpose, and greatness over every child. Today, Grace Horizon International School continues to nurture that vision, raising confident, compassionate, and exceptional young minds who are prepared to make a meaningful difference in the world.

Dr. Samuel & Nike Ajayi Founders, Grace Horizon International School
